## Problem
Moto‑taxi passengers in Musanze face unpredictable fares and lack a safety check‑in system. Without benchmarks, riders risk being overcharged, and families have no way to confirm safe arrival. This creates stress, financial loss, and safety concerns for the community.

## Solution
Smart TaxiPark provides a digital fare benchmark and a simple check‑in feature. Passengers can see fair prices before riding, and families receive instant confirmation when riders arrive safely. This builds trust, reduces disputes, and improves community safety.

## Value
Passengers gain transparency, families gain peace of mind, and the community benefits from safer, fairer transport — right now, when demand for reliable moto‑taxi services is growing.
